Cat artwork was generated with OpenAI imagegen using the user's supplied cat
photograph as the identity reference. The original photograph is not embedded
in the executable. The optional purr is a quiet synthesized sound, not an
audio recording of the user's cat.

All care behavior, settings and saves are local. There is no application
telemetry, runtime web service, login, or network-dependent functionality.
